My research lies at the intersection of applied mathematics, mathematical biology, and scientific computing, with a particular focus on reaction–diffusion systems and nonlinear partial differential equations.

I am interested in understanding how diffusion, geometry, and biochemical interactions give rise to spatial and temporal pattern formation in biological systems.

This simulation illustrates the emergence and evolution of spatio-temporal patterns in a reaction–diffusion system defined on an annular domain. It demonstrates how diffusion, nonlinear interactions, and domain geometry can jointly shape complex spatial organization over time.
